# MANDATORY WRITING STYLE RULE

- **Tone & Style:** Direct, concise, technical, human Product Lead style.
- **NO FLUFF / NO AI JARGON:** Never use flowery, overly poetic, or generic AI buzzwords (e.g., "bệ phóng bứt phá", "khẳng định vị thế", "hành trình chuyển đổi", "bứt phá ngoạn mục").
- **Format:** Focus strictly on facts, metrics, actionable bullet points, and code/architecture rationale.
