1. Phat nguoi la gi?

Phat nguoi la hinh thuc xu phat vi pham hanh chinh trong linh vuc giao thong duong bo, trong do co quan chuc nang ghi nhan hanh vi vi pham thong qua he thong camera giam sat hoac thiet bi ky thuat nghiep vu, sau do gui thong bao xu phat den chu phuong tien ma khong can dung xe tai thoi diem vi pham.

Theo Nghi dinh 168/2024/ND-CP, tu ngay 01/01/2025, he thong camera phat nguoi da duoc lap dat tren toan bo 63 tinh thanh voi hon 15.000 thiet bi giam sat, tang 300% so voi nam 2023.

Luu y: Phat nguoi co thoi hieu xu phat toi da 2 nam ke tu ngay vi pham. Sau thoi han nay, co quan chuc nang khong duoc phep ra quyet dinh xu phat.

2. Cach tra cuu phat nguoi online

Hien nay co 3 kenh chinh de tra cuu phat nguoi truc tuyen hoan toan mien phi:

  1. Cong Dich vu Cong Quoc gia: Truy cap dichvucong.gov.vn → Muc "Tra cuu phat nguoi" → Nhap bien so xe.
  2. Ung dung MoMo: Mo App MoMo → Dich vu cong → Tra cuu phat nguoi → Nhap bien so → Xem ket qua va nop phat ngay.
  3. Website Cuc CSGT: Truy cap csgt.vn → Muc "Tra cuu vi pham".

3. Muc phat chi tiet theo loai phuong tien

Muc phat nguoi duoc quy dinh tai Nghi dinh 168/2024/ND-CP, ap dung tu 01/01/2025. Duoi day la bang tong hop cac muc phat pho bien nhat theo loai phuong tien:

3.1. Xe may

Cac loi vi pham pho bien nhat doi voi xe may bao gom: vuot den do (800.000d - 1.000.000d), di nguoc chieu (1.000.000d - 2.000.000d), va khong doi mu bao hiem (400.000d - 600.000d).

3.2. O to

Doi voi o to, muc phat nghiem khac hon dang ke: vuot den do (4.000.000d - 6.000.000d), vuot toc do tren 35km/h (10.000.000d - 12.000.000d), va di vao duong cam (3.000.000d - 5.000.000d).

4. Huong dan nop phat nguoi qua MoMo

MoMo la vi dien tu dau tien tai Viet Nam ho tro nop phat nguoi giao thong truc tuyen. Quy trinh chi mat 2 phut:

  1. Mo ung dung MoMo → Tim kiem "Phat nguoi"
  2. Nhap bien so xe → He thong hien thi cac khoan phat chua nop
  3. Chon khoan phat can nop → Xac nhan thanh toan
  4. Nhan bien lai dien tu ngay lap tuc

5. Cau hoi thuong gap (FAQ)

Q: Phat nguoi co bi tuoc bang lai khong?

A: Co. Mot so loi vi pham nghiem trong nhu vuot toc do tren 35km/h hoac di nguoc chieu tren cao toc co the bi tuoc giay phep lai xe tu 2-4 thang.

Q: Nop phat nguoi qua MoMo co duoc giam phi khong?

A: MoMo mien phi dich vu tra cuu va nop phat. Nguoi dung chi thanh toan dung so tien phat theo quyet dinh xu phat.
