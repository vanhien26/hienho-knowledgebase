## Q2 2026 DECISIONS

### Decision 1: Shift Traffic Fines (Phạt Nguội) to Utilities-Led SEO Approach

**Date**: 2026-04-27
**Project**: Phạt Nguội Mini-app
**Status**: In-Progress

#### Context
- **Background**: Traffic Fines mini-app launched with direct traffic strategy but facing low conversion and high CAC
- **Current State**: Running ads, getting clicks, but not converting efficiently
- **Trigger**: Q2 planning + market insight about search behavior + team capacity availability
- **Constraints**: 
  - Web Platform available only in Q2 (busy in Q3 with MoSpark)
  - Limited content team bandwidth
  - CMS transition happening May-June
- **Stakeholders**: Klaus (strategy), Web Platform lead (execution), Content team (content), Product (metrics)

#### Options Considered

**Option A: Continue Direct Traffic Approach**
- **How it works**: Increase ad spend, refine targeting, push app installs
- **Pros**: Familiar approach, quick learnings
- **Cons**: Diminishing returns observed, high CAC, dependent on ad budget
- **Effort**: High (ongoing ad management + optimization)
- **Risk**: Budget escalation without guaranteed ROI improvement

**Option B: Utilities-Led SEO Approach (CHOSEN)**
- **How it works**: Build content hub around "how to pay traffic fines", solve user intent, then promote mini-app as solution
- **Pros**: 
  - Better user intent match (users search "how to pay", not "traffic fines app")
  - Leverages GPD SEO strength
  - Lower CAC through organic discovery
  - Aligns with MoMo GEO strategy (appear in AI engines)
- **Cons**: Takes longer (3-4 months to see impact), requires content planning, dependent on Web Platform roadmap
- **Effort**: Medium-High (strategy planning + content hub building + tracking setup)
- **Risk**: Content hub performance uncertainty

**Option C: Embed in MoMo Ecosystem**
- **How it works**: Cross-promote from existing products (Credit, Insurance, Payment)
- **Pros**: Uses existing traffic, bundled offering
- **Cons**: 
  - Depends on other product roadmaps (not in our control)
  - Limited distribution channels
  - Timing misalignment
- **Effort**: Medium (coordination)
- **Risk**: Execution delay, not owner of timeline

#### Decision: Option B (Utilities-Led SEO)

**Reasoning**:
Traffic fines is utility-driven search behavior. User journey: "How to pay traffic fines?" → Search → Discover → Solution (mini-app). This matches MoMo's GEO North Star (appear in Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity for finance queries). 

The Web Platform has capacity in Q2, content team is available, and this aligns with company direction. It's a better ROI story than direct ads + has strategic value beyond this project.

**Trade-offs Accepted**:
- Longer timeline to impact (3-4 months vs ads weeks)
- Higher dependency on Web Platform execution
- CMS transition timing risk

**Assumptions**:
- Search intent exists for "how to pay traffic fines"
- Web Platform can build content hub in Q2
- Content-to-app conversion will be >5%
- Users searching for utilities will also have traffic fines (high overlap)

#### Execution

- **Owner**: Klaus (strategy) + Web Platform Lead (technical)
- **Team**: Content team, GTM team (tracking)
- **Timeline**: 
  - Content strategy: Due 2026-05-15
  - Content hub MVP: Due 2026-06-30
  - First data: 2026-07-31
- **Success Criteria**:
  - 100 ranked keywords for traffic fines utilities
  - 5%+ CTR to mini-app from content
  - <$2 CAC from organic

#### Review Date
- **Check-in**: 2026-07-15 (mid-Q3 after some data)
- **Kill-Switch**: If by 2026-09-30 we have <500 monthly traffic or <2% conversion, pivot back to ads
